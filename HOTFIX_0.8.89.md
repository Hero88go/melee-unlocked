## Install

Download `MeleeUnlocked-0.8.89-win64.zip` from this release. Close the game and launcher, then extract it over your existing Melee Unlocked folder. Settings, saves, mods and replays stay in place. Use your own Melee NTSC 1.02 ISO.

## Fixes

- Stability, Static Recomp: every match left a little of the game's stack in use, and a long session ended in a crash (stack overflow) while the next screen loaded. Matches no longer leave anything behind.
- Display: new CRT display option under Video (Studio monitor or Home television). It draws scanline beams, a phosphor mask sized to your screen and a little glow. Off by default.
- Launcher: the Slippi sign-in is also found when the Slippi Launcher keeps its Dolphin in another folder, and the line under Play says what is missing (no Slippi Launcher, no sign-in, or no connect code yet).
- Controllers: when another program has the GameCube adapter open, the log says so, and names Steam when Steam is running.
