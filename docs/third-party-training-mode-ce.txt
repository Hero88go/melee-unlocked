Training Mode - Community Edition

The Source Port runs Training Mode CE's events natively: its C source is compiled into
melee_game.dll, and the game-side changes its PowerPC patches make are written as C at the same
points in the game's source. Its disc files (the TM folder: menus, the Lab, event data) are not
included; they load from your own Training Mode CE disc image.

Training Mode CE by UnclePunch and contributors.
https://github.com/AlexanderHarrison/TrainingMode-CommunityEdition
