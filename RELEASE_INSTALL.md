## Install - launcher or BAT

Download and extract `MeleeUnlocked-<version>-win64.zip`, then choose either:

- **Launcher:** Open `MeleeUnlockedLauncher.exe`, drop your Melee NTSC 1.02 ISO onto the Build tab, then press **PLAY**.
- **BAT (no launcher needed):** Drag your ISO onto `MeleeUnlocked.bat`. Alternatively, put it beside the BAT as `melee.iso` and double-click the BAT.

The launcher is optional. You supply your own ISO. Press **F1** in game for PC settings.
