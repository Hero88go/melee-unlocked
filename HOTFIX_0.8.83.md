## Install

Download `MeleeUnlocked-0.8.83-win64.zip` from this release. Close the game and launcher, then extract it over your existing Melee Unlocked folder. Settings, saves, mods and replays stay in place. Use your own Melee NTSC 1.02 ISO.

## New

- Send recent game logs (launcher, Play page): describe what happened and send your game log, lobby log and the online matches you marked. Names, accounts, addresses and folder names are removed first.
- Marking a problem online: press D-pad Left (looked wrong), D-pad Right (input wrong or late) or D-pad Down (sounded wrong) the moment it happens. Only matches you marked are kept and sent.
- Launcher: the Play page shows which mods are active.

## Fixes

- Source Port: fixed a crash during the staff roll after Classic and All-Star. The highlighted credits change color again.
- Sub-frame interpolation now stays on while the pause camera zooms in and out, instead of dropping for 2 to 3 seconds.
