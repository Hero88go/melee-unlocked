## Install

Download `MeleeUnlocked-0.8.86-win64.zip` from this release. Close the game and launcher, then extract it over your existing Melee Unlocked folder. Settings, saves, mods and replays stay in place. Use your own Melee NTSC 1.02 ISO.

## Fixes

- Graphics: on a laptop with two graphics chips the game now runs on the dedicated one. It could start on the integrated chip, which caused flicker and a low frame rate.
