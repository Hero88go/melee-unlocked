## Install

Download `MeleeUnlocked-0.8.87-win64.zip` from this release. Close the game and launcher, then extract it over your existing Melee Unlocked folder. Settings, saves, mods and replays stay in place. Use your own Melee NTSC 1.02 ISO.

## Fixes

- Mods: the skin list showed a grey figure for most costumes. Every costume now shows a picture of the fighter wearing it, for your skins and for the standard costumes. The pictures are drawn from the costume files the first time the list is opened.
